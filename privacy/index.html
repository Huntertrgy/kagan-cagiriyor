Privacy Policy: The Khagan Calls: Road to Ötüken (Kağan Çağırıyor: Ötüken'e Doğru)

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

App: The Khagan Calls: Road to Ötüken / Kağan Çağırıyor: Ötüken'e Doğru (on-device app name "The Khagan Calls", or "Kağan Çağırıyor" on Turkish devices; Android, package com.conaksoft.otukenedogru)

Publisher and data controller: Conak Soft LLC, 1209 Mountain Road Pl NE Ste N, Albuquerque, NM 87110-7845, USA · https://conaksoft.com/ · conaksoft@gmail.com

1. In short

  • We do not ask for an account, name, email, phone number or location permission. Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The game contains ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob and ad partners, which may use the advertising ID and similar device information (section 4).
  • If you arrived through the link of one of our promotional partners, the user ID that partner supplied and the progress level you reach may be sent to that partner (section 5).
  • The game is designed for players aged 13 and over.
  • For questions about your data write to conaksoft@gmail.com.

2. What we do not collect

As publisher we do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, address, precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or payment card details. The game has no account, sign-in, cloud save, chat or leaderboard.

The app's Android permissions are: internet access, network state, vibration and the advertising ID (AD_ID). It asks for nothing else.

3. Game data stored on your device

The game stores the following only on your device: progress (camp, chapter, Flame and Ember, campaign state), settings (language, sound, camera mode, sensitivity, camp selection), hints already shown, the hero you chose and the name you gave your hero (kept only on your device, shared with no one), and backups of these.

This data is not sent to us. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings; clearing deletes your progress and it cannot be restored.

4. Ads (Google AdMob and ad partners)

The game shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional. Ads are served through Google AdMob; AdMob may use mediation with the following ad networks:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC; Google Ireland Limited in the EEA/UK)
  • Pangle (Pangle / ByteDance group)
  • Liftoff (Liftoff Mobile, Inc.; Vungle)

To serve, measure and secure ads these SDKs may collect: the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device model, operating system and app version, language and time zone, ad impressions, clicks and viewing interactions, and crash and performance information. Partners process this data under their own privacy policies. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Consent and choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require consent, Google's consent form (UMP) appears and asks for your choice about personalized ads. You can change it later with the Privacy Options button in the game's Settings screen (shown where required).

In US states, Google's applicable privacy message and choices apply.

To reset or delete the advertising ID and turn off personalization on your device: Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads). If the advertising ID is deleted, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.

Ad requests use the general 13+ audience setting with a "parental guidance" content limit. The game is not directed to children under 13 (section 8).

5. Promotional partners and source information

On first launch the game reads, once, through the Google Play Install Referrer service, which link your install came from (link parameters such as source name and campaign name).

  • If you came directly from Google Play or from our own links, the device keeps only a note that the source was read; nothing is sent to anyone.
  • If you came through a promotional partner's link, the user ID the partner added to the link and the source name are stored on your device. When you reach certain progress levels (camp count), the following fields may be sent to the partner's server: user ID, progress level reached, app package name, timestamp and an integrity signature. Before sending, a request is made to Google's connectivity check address to get the correct time (your IP address is visible to Google).
  • No other personal data (name, location, advertising ID, device ID) is included in this transmission.
  • This data is subject to the partner's own privacy policy; the partner manages your relationship with them and your user ID.

Using a promotional partner's link is entirely your choice; if you install directly from Google Play, none of this sharing happens.

6. Analytics and tracking

The game does not use any third-party analytics or user-tracking SDK. We see aggregated, non-identifying statistics that Google Play provides in Google Play Console (installs, crashes/ANRs, device types). Ad partners may run their own measurement within the scope of section 4.

7. Sharing and sale of data

We share data only with: (a) the ad partners in section 4, (b) the promotional partners in section 5 (only for players who came through a partner link), and (c) authorities where legally required. We do not otherwise sell your personal data for money.

8. Children

The game is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes their child uses the game can write to conaksoft@gmail.com; we recommend uninstalling the app, and we will delete any related data we hold.

9. Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Because we hold no account or profile for you, there is no record on our servers to delete. Data held by ad partners and promotional partners is subject to their own policies and retention periods.

10. Security

Network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS). The progress notification sent to partners carries an integrity signature. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

11. International transfers

The publisher is in the USA; ad and promotional partners may process data in many countries. For transfers from the EEA/UK, the mechanisms used by the partners (for example standard contractual clauses) apply.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (KVKK article 11 in Turkey; GDPR in the EEA/UK; applicable laws in US states) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to processing and port your data.

Because we hold no identifiable account for you in the game, most data sits on your device; for the advertising ID and ad data you can use the relevant partner's tools (section 4) and your device settings. To make a request write to conaksoft@gmail.com (we reply within 30 days). You may also complain to your data protection authority.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. We announce material changes on this page and, where needed, in the game's release notes; the "Last updated" date is refreshed.
